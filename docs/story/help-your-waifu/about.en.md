### How a run goes

1. **Claim a base.** Walk onto a free plot and a red house builds itself on it. New players get a short 4-step guide with a glowing beam that points the way.
2. **Run the obby.** A fresh obby is built every 5 minutes. Level 1 is open; levels 2 to 8 sit behind walls you buy with cash, from $500 up to $300K.
3. **Grab a mystery waifu.** Waifus wait at the end of side paths as black silhouettes that only show their rarity. Who she really is gets rolled the moment you pick her up. Picking one up costs 30 seconds of her income (Commons are free).
4. **Carry her home.** You can't just put her down. Fall or die and she goes back to her square, and the pickup price is gone. Magic Carpet owners can fly home instead.
5. **Move her in, or sell her.** Click your house to move her in, or sell her for 60 seconds of her income.
6. **Let her work.** Waifus at your base stroll around the yard, emote, say lines and earn cash every second, even while you're away (50% of your income, for up to 8 hours).

### Rarities

The deeper the level, the rarer the waifu. There are 33 to collect.

- **Common** (levels 1–2): $5 a second
- **Rare** (levels 3–4): $15 a second
- **Epic** (levels 5–6): $50 a second
- **Legendary** (level 7): $150 a second
- **Mythic** (level 8): $500 a second

### Events and extras

- **Legendary event:** every 10 minutes, for 90 seconds, Legendaries show up on levels 1–6, each with a 30% chance of being a Mythic.
- **Luck Boost:** every waifu gets a 35% chance to roll one rarity higher.
- **Lootbox shop:** three boxes ($10K, $100K and $1M) holding 18 emotes and 13 auras to dress up your waifus. Every box shows its odds, the whole server shares one stock that restocks every 5 minutes, and duplicates refund half.
- **Getting around:** quick travel, sprint, and a phone layout, so it plays on any device.
