### Run an obby that rebuilds itself

![Running a freshly built obby](assets/projects/help-your-waifu.webp)

- A brand-new obby every 5 minutes, built from hand-made pieces
- 8 levels: the deeper you go, the rarer the waifus
- Unlock the harder levels with the cash you earn

### Carry her home

![Carrying a waifu back to the base](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/about-carry.webp)

- Grab a mystery waifu at the end of a side path
- Fall on the way and she runs back to her spot
- Bring her home and she moves into your base

### Collect them all

![Dressing up a waifu, then the waifus walking around the base](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/about-collect.webp)

- 33 waifus in 5 rarities, from Common to Mythic
- They earn cash every second, even while you're offline
- Dress them up with emotes and auras from loot boxes
