### Obby, waifus and bases

![A player's base: a red-roofed house on a green yard, with waifus walking around](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/base-redhouse-yard.jpg)

- An obby generator that rebuilds 8 levels every 5 minutes
- Mystery waifus with rarity rolls, carrying and piggyback climbing
- Bases where waifus roam the yard and earn cash
- Income, offline earnings, selling and the Legendary event

### Shop, saves and admin

![The Waifu Shop: loot boxes with their stock, price and rarity odds](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/shop-scrolling-rows.jpg)

- Lootboxes, 4 game passes and 7 developer products
- ProfileStore saves, and Robux receipts that never pay out twice
- An admin panel with 38 commands and live events
- One shared UI kit behind every screen, made to work on phones
