### 1. The core loop

![The core loop: run the obby, grab a waifu, carry her home, move her in, she earns cash, unlock deeper levels, repeat](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/board/01-core-loop.en.webp)

- Run the obby, grab a waifu and carry her home
- Move her in, let her earn cash, unlock deeper levels
- Then repeat, with rarer waifus every lap

### 2. Progression

![Progression table: levels 1 to 8 with their difficulty, waifu rarity, income and unlock cost](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/board/02-progression.en.webp)

- Level 1 is open; levels 2 to 8 sit behind cash walls, $500 to $300K
- Deeper levels are harder and hold rarer waifus
- Income climbs from $5 a second (Common) to $500 (Mythic)

### 3. Map and base

![The obby as a tall strip of levels 1 to 8 with mystery waifus on side paths, and the base going from a row of slots to a house with a yard](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/board/03-map-base.en.webp)

- One tall obby: start at the bottom, levels 1 to 8 above
- Mystery waifus wait at the end of side paths
- The base went from a row of slots to a house with a yard

### 4. How carrying works

![Carrying flow: pick her up, carry her, then either reach your base or fall and lose her](assets/projects/help-your-waifu/board/04-carrying.en.webp)

- Press E to pick her up; it costs 30 seconds of her income
- Reach your base to move her in or sell her
- Fall or die, and she goes back to her spot
