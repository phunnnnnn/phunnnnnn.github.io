### Ten days in August

- Built in 10 days in August 2026, synced into Roblox Studio with Rojo
- Aug 5: the full loop, from seed belt to seller and leaderboard
- Aug 6: 18 seeds, factories, a five-tier refining ladder and NPC workers
- Aug 7 and 14: generator factories, belt levels and an upgrade shop

### Workers do it all

- Workers run fully on the server, so there's nothing to exploit
- Each one harvests, refines and sells, and the coins go to its owner
- They're real R6 rigs, built part by part
- Their walk follows the distance travelled, so their legs stop when they stop

### Upgrades are just data

- A generator track is a name, a price list and three functions
- Adding a new one is one more entry in a table
