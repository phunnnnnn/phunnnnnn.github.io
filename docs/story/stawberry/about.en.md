### How it plays

1. **Buy a seed.** Seeds come down a single-file conveyor belt, one at a time. There are 18 kinds, from a cheap apple to a pricey watermelon, and the rarer ones glow in their rarity's colour.
2. **Plant it.** Hold the seed and click your farm. A disc follows your cursor: green means the seed will land there, red tells you why it won't (too close to another plant, off the edge, too far away).
3. **Harvest.** When the fruit is ripe, press E to pick it. The plant keeps bearing.
4. **Refine it.** Bring fruit to a factory you placed on your own grid to turn it into pies, and pies up a five-tier ladder of better products. Every machine shows its tier three ways: by colour, by a row of roof pips, and on its prompt.
5. **Sell.** Take it all to the seller. Hold up an item to see its price card: unit price, how many you carry and what the lot is worth. Coins are ranked on the leaderboard.

### Growing the farm

- **Workers:** a rotating pool of NPC candidates stands around, each with its own walk speed and a price to match. Hire one and it farms, refines and sells for you on its own.
- **Generators:** levelled factories come from your own generator, and the seed belt has levels too. Both are bought in an upgrade shop that shows the exact odds.
- **The first choice:** a seed costs four harvests of its own fruit, so you open with either a row of fast, cheap plants or one or two slow, rich ones.
