### The gameplay loop

![Stawberry's loop: buy a seed, plant it, pick the fruit, refine it at factories, sell for coins, upgrade and hire](assets/projects/stawberry/loop.en.webp)

- Plant, pick, refine and sell, then spend the coins to farm faster
- Every lap, the farm earns a little more, so each lap is faster

### Buy and plant

![Buying a seed from the conveyor belt](assets/projects/stawberry/about-buy.webp)

- Seeds roll in on a conveyor belt, one at a time
- 18 kinds, from a cheap apple to a pricey watermelon
- Equip a seed and click your farm to plant it

### Pick, refine and sell

![Plants growing fruit, and the player picking it](assets/projects/stawberry/about-harvest.webp)

- Press E to pick ripe fruit, and the plant bears again
- Factories you place turn fruit into pies, up a five-tier ladder
- Sell to the seller, and coins are ranked on the leaderboard

### Grow the farm

![A fly-over of the farm, the seed belt, the workers and the factory area](assets/projects/stawberry/about-farm.webp)

- Hire NPC workers who farm, refine and sell for you
- Win levelled factories from your own generator
- The upgrade shop shows the exact odds
