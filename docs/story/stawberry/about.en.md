### Plant and pick

- Seeds roll in on a conveyor belt, one at a time
- 18 kinds, from a cheap apple to a pricey watermelon
- Plant them on your farm, then press E to pick ripe fruit

### Refine and sell

- Factories you place turn fruit into pies
- Pies climb a five-tier ladder of better products
- Sell to the seller, and coins are ranked on the leaderboard

### Grow the farm

- Hire NPC workers who farm, refine and sell for you
- Win levelled factories from your own generator
- The upgrade shop shows the exact odds
- A seed costs four harvests, so start fast and cheap or slow and rich
