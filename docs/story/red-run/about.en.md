### Aim, bounce, paint

- Aim the brush, and a preview shows its first bounces
- Release and watch it ricochet, splashing red on every wall hit
- Then pick your next angle from where it stopped

### Canvas by canvas

- Each canvas takes a few shots, about 30 to 60 seconds
- More coverage pays more coins, plus a bonus for unused shots on a Perfect
- After each canvas, buy one of three upgrades in the shop
- Every third canvas is a Critic canvas with one special rule

### A full run

- 8 galleries of 3 canvases, about 20 to 25 minutes
- Build toward the one perfect shot that floods the whole screen

### Where it's headed

- First release planned for PC (Steam) and the web
- Mobile comes after launch
- Single-player, made for players who love finishing things perfectly
