### The core loop

**Moment to moment:** aim the brush (a preview shows its first bounces), release, and watch it ricochet. Every wall hit splashes, the red trail spreads and the coverage climbs. Then pick the next angle from where it stopped.

**Canvas to canvas:** each canvas takes a few shots, about 30 to 60 seconds. The coverage you reach pays coins in tiers (more painted, more coins), plus a bonus for every unused shot on a Perfect. Then the shop: buy one of three upgrades, and on to the next canvas. Every third canvas is a Critic canvas with one special rule, like sticky walls or white paint creeping back.

**A run:** 8 galleries of 3 canvases, about 20 to 25 minutes, building toward the one perfect shot that floods the whole screen.

### Where it's headed

The first release is planned for PC (Steam) and the web, with mobile after launch. It's single-player, made for players who love finishing things perfectly.
