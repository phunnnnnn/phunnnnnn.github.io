### The gameplay loop

![Harvest the Dept loop: drag a seed bag, drop it on a pot, press Next Day, watch it grow](assets/projects/harvest-the-dept/loop.en.webp)

- Plant a seed, then end the day to make it grow
- Four days take a seed to a full sunflower

### Plant and grow

![Dragging a seed bag into a pot, then pressing Next Day until a sunflower blooms](assets/projects/harvest-the-dept/clip.webp)

- Drag a seed bag with the mouse; the target pot lights up
- Press Next Day and every planted pot grows a stage
