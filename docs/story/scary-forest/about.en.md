### Ready up together

![The lobby counting down, then the arena opening](assets/projects/scary-forest/about-lobby.webp)

- Step on the ready pad in the lobby
- A 10-second countdown sends everyone in at once
- The countdown stops if a player leaves

### Hold off the zombies

![Fighting zombies in the arena](assets/projects/scary-forest.webp)

- Zombies keep coming and chase the players
- Every kill earns points toward your high score

### Last one standing

![The last player falls and the round starts over in the lobby](assets/projects/scary-forest/about-reset.webp)

- The round lasts until the last player falls
- Then everyone is back in the lobby for the next round
