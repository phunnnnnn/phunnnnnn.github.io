### What happens on a pop

![The explode() function in GDScript](assets/projects/ballon/code-1.webp)

- The combo goes up by one, capped by the Combo Pressure upgrade
- Gold paid is the Inflation Rate × combo × the balloon's multiplier

### Spawning balloons

![The spawner code that places balloons and rolls their type](assets/projects/ballon/code-2.webp)

- Balloons appear at random spots in the play area
- Each one rolls its type from the Elite Balloons odds
