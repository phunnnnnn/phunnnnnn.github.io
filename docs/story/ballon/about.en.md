### The gameplay loop

![Ballon loop: pop balloons, build your combo, earn gold, buy upgrades](assets/projects/ballon/loop.en.webp)

- Every pop pays gold, multiplied by your combo
- Rarer elite balloons pay up to 100 times more

### Pop for gold

![Popping balloons fast with the combo at its limit](assets/projects/ballon/clip.webp)

- A new balloon floats in every 1.3 seconds
- Each pop raises the combo, up to its limit
- Upgrades raise the limit, and some pop balloons for you
