### How a round goes

1. **Sweep the kelp.** Swim over kelp to harvest it; kelp is your money. It regrows at the start of every wave, and new plants keep sprouting.
2. **Survive the wave.** Enemies (chasers, dashers and shooters) arrive from the edges. You can't fight, but clicking an enemy sends every pet after it.
3. **Use your pets' skills.** Every 8 seconds each pet drops a token. Swim over it to fire that pet's skill.
4. **Shop between waves.** A cleared wave opens the shop: buy new pets (each one costs more than the last) and items from a 3×3 grid you can refresh, then drag items onto your pets.
5. **Beat the bosses.** A round is 3 waves: a Bloat Puffer mini boss shows up in wave 2, and wave 3 is a boss, announced by an intro card.

When your HP hits zero, a stats card shows how you did and your best wave is saved.

### 20 pets, 5 rarities

Seven Common, five Rare, four Epic, two Legendary and two Mythic pets, rolled at 55 / 25 / 12 / 6 / 2 %. Rarer pets hit harder with their skills, up to 2.5 times as hard. Every kill gives every pet XP, up to level 50. A few of the skills:

- **Electric Eel:** chain lightning that stuns.
- **Pistol Shrimp:** a shockwave.
- **Frost Jelly:** a freeze that shatters.

### 5 bosses

- **Shark:** the first test.
- **Hermit Crab Titan:** takes only 10% damage while it hides in its shell.
- **Storm Eel:** only its head can be hit.
- **Kraken:** slams the arena in lanes, grabs your pets and blacks out the screen.
- **Abyss Angler:** darkens the arena and drops fake tokens.

Every fast attack is warned first and locked to a line, so a hit always feels fair.

### Play it

Lost Dept is out on itch.io, in the browser or on Windows.
