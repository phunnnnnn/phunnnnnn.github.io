### Sweep the kelp

![Sweeping the mouse over kelp to collect it](assets/projects/lost-dept/about-kelp.webp)

- Kelp is your money: sweep your mouse over it to collect it
- It grows back every wave
- You can't fight, so every kelp counts

### Your pets fight for you

![Pet fish attacking an enemy](assets/projects/lost-dept/about-pets.webp)

- 20 pets in 5 rarities, each with its own skill
- Click an enemy and every pet goes after it
- Buy pets and items in the shop between waves

### Beat the bosses

![Fighting the Kraken](assets/projects/lost-dept.webp)

- 5 bosses, each with its own trick
- Every big attack is warned first, so a hit always feels fair
- Out now on itch.io, in the browser or on Windows
