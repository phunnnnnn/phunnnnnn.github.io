### Pets and skills

![The pixel art pets and items](assets/projects/lost-dept/pets.jpg)

- 20 pets, each with its own skill, rarity and level
- Every pet picks its next move ten times a second

### Waves and bosses

![Intro card for the Abyss Angler boss](assets/projects/lost-dept/boss-intro.jpg)

- Enemy HP is set from the damage your team should deal
- Five bosses with their own attack cycles and intro cards

### Shop and game feel

![The shop with items and pet cards](assets/projects/lost-dept/shop.jpg)

- Drag items onto pets, and pay to refresh the shop
- Screen shake, and hit-stop on big impacts
- Water and light shaders, a tutorial and arrows to off-screen enemies

### Testing

- 15 automated tests
- A bot that plays the game to balance it
