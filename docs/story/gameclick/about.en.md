### The gameplay loop

![GameClick loop: click the button, the bomb bites back, grab upgrades, survive the clock](assets/projects/gameclick/loop.en.webp)

- Click faster than the bomb takes your clicks away
- Last five minutes to finish the run

### Click against the bomb

![Clicking while the bomb grows stronger, then taking an upgrade card](assets/projects/gameclick/clip.webp)

- Every click adds to your count and your money
- The bomb starts after 5 seconds and gets stronger every 5
- Shop cards add click power, and you can reroll for more
