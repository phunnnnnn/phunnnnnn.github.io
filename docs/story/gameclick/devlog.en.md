### The clock and the bomb

![The timer, bomb and game-over code in GDScript](assets/projects/gameclick/code-1.webp)

- The clock ticks every 0.1 seconds, for 300 seconds
- The bomb hits every second and gains 1 power every 5
- The run ends when the count drops below 0 or time runs out

### The shop pauses time

![The shop code: pausing, resuming and rerolling](assets/projects/gameclick/code-2.webp)

- Opening the shop pauses every timer
- Reroll adds another upgrade card
- Leaving the shop starts the timers again
