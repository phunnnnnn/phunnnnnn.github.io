### How a match goes

1. **Find a room.** In the lobby, create a room and share its 6-character code, join a friend's code, or hit Quick Play. The host picks the mode and starts, everyone confirms, and the whole party is teleported into its own match server.
2. **Plan.** Each round opens with 30 seconds of planning. Buy units from your 5-slot shop, reroll it for 2 gold, or buy XP for 4 gold to level up (up to level 10) and field more units. Three copies of a unit merge into a stronger star level.
3. **Place.** Drag units onto your hex board. The server checks every placement.
4. **Fight.** Armies fight on their own until one side is wiped out, and you can still hand items to your units mid-fight.
5. **Resolve.** A loss costs 2 HP for every enemy unit still standing. Everyone starts with 100 HP, and the last player alive wins.

### Rounds and stages

Stage 1 is three fights against monsters with a carousel round in the middle, where everyone picks up a prize. From stage 2 on, each stage has a monster round, a carousel and three fights against other players. With an odd number of players, one of them fights a ghost copy of someone else's board.

### Economy

Every round pays 5 gold, plus 1 for every 10 gold you've saved (up to 5 more), a bonus for win or loss streaks, and 1 more for a win. Selling a unit gives its full price back. Each unit has a limited number of copies in a pool the whole match shares (30 for 1-cost units, down to 9 for 5-costs), so what other players buy changes what you can find.

### What's in it so far

57 units across five costs (38 with skills so far), 25 traits (15 working so far), 8 item components and 36 completed items, 7 augments, an 8-player mode, a solo mode against monsters, and a sandbox for testing skills.
