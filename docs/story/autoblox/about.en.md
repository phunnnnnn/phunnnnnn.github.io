### Build your army

![Placing units on the hex board](assets/projects/autoblox/about-plan.webp)

- Buy units from a shop that every player shares
- Place them on your hex board before each fight
- Three copies of a unit merge into a stronger one

### Watch them fight

![Two armies fighting on the board](assets/projects/autoblox.webp)

- Armies fight on their own until one side falls
- Hand items to your units, even mid-fight
- The server runs every fight, so nobody can cheat

### Play with friends

- Make a room and share its code, or hit Quick Play
- Up to 8 players, and the last one standing wins
- Still in development: 57 units and 25 traits so far
